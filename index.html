Frequently Asked Questions

Is the driver allowance included in the price?
No, the driver allowance is usually ₹300/day, paid directly to the driver. However, for long tours (5+ days), we often negotiate this into the package. Ask us for details!
Do you provide AC in all cars?
Yes, all our vehicles are fully AC. However, in extreme cold (Spiti/January), we recommend keeping windows slightly open for fresh air if the AC feels too strong.
What is the advance booking payment policy?
We generally require a 20-30% advance deposit to confirm your booking, with the remaining balance payable directly to the driver during the trip.
Can we customize our Himachal tour itinerary?
Yes! All our tours are fully customizable. You can add or remove destinations, sightseeing spots, and stopovers according to your preference.
